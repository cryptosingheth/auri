Reference — fees, limits and parameters

Every fee, minimum, limit and parameter Auri applies, in one place. Figures are taken from the V2.2 product build, and lending parameters are cross-checked against the live on-chain market. Where a figure is not defined anywhere in the product, this page says UNKNOWN rather than guessing. Nothing here is financial, tax or legal advice.

Percentage fees are currency-agnostic and apply the same everywhere. Flat amounts and reward values are shown in the currency of the build they came from — the V2.2 build is CAD-denominated, and Interac rails are Canada-only. Live gold price, the live borrow rate and your own liquidation level are always read from the app, never from this page.

Two fee gaps you should be aware of. The build never states a fee for bank/EFT deposits, bank withdrawals or wallet withdrawals, and it never states an Auri Card fee — yet the Jewels catalogue sells a "waive card fee" reward, which implies at least one card fee exists. Treat all four as undefined until the schedule is published.

How these figures can change

Three different kinds of number appear on this page, and they change in three different ways.

Auri's own fees and minimums — the 0.49% buy and sell fee, the 1% debit-card fee, the $10 Auto-Invest minimum, the Jewels and referral rates. Auri sets these and can change them. Percentages hold across currencies; flat amounts and reward values are set per currency and per region.

Lending parameters — the borrow rate and the 77% liquidation level. Auri does not set either. The rate is produced continuously by the lending market's interest-rate model in response to how much of the supplied USDC is borrowed, which is why it moves during a single day and why no page should ever describe it as fixed. The liquidation level is fixed in the market's contract; a different level would mean a different market. Both are readable on chain by anyone, at the link above.

Availability — which rails and features exist for you depends on where you live. Interac e-Transfer is Canada-only; in the US the equivalent is a bank transfer. Debit-card funding, the card, remittance corridors and tax exports are all regional. Nothing on this page guarantees a feature is available to your account — the app shows you what your account can actually do.

When this page and the app disagree, the app is right. It reads live prices, your live health factor and your live liquidation price. This page is a reference for parameters, not a quote. New here? Start at Getting started.
